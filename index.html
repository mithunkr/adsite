Financial Secrets

Money knowledge that quietly pays for itself.

HSA

The only triple-tax-free account in existence

A Health Savings Account is pre-tax going in, grows tax-free, and comes out tax-free for medical costs — three advantages no IRA or 401(k) matches. 2025 limits: $4,300 solo / $8,550 family. After 65 it works like a second IRA.

Retirement

The 4% withdrawal rule quietly became 3.3%

Bengen's original 4% rule assumed a 30-year retirement. For modern 40+ year retirements (retiring at 55–60), updated research points to 3.3% as the safer inflation-adjusted withdrawal rate.

Medical Bills

You can negotiate medical bills after insurance pays

Hospitals carry a high "chargemaster" price and a lower accepted price. Studies show 78% of patients who simply ask for a reduction receive one — averaging 35–50% off. Request the itemized bill first; up to 80% contain errors.

Legal Rights You Already Have

Rights that exist right now — most people just never use them.

Employment

You can inspect your own personnel file

In over 30 US states, employees have a statutory right to review their employer's personnel file — performance reviews, disciplinary records, hiring notes. Crucial information before any dispute or termination.

Consumer

EU261: up to €600 for delayed flights, any nationality

If a flight to or from any EU airport is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, you're owed €250–€600 per passenger — regardless of citizenship, and even on US airlines flying EU routes. Most travelers never claim it.

Debt

Collectors can't call before 8am or after 9pm

The Fair Debt Collection Practices Act forbids it. Send a written cease-and-desist and all contact must stop. Violations carry $1,000 in statutory damages per incident, collectible in small claims court.

Health Facts Science Confirms

Evidence-based and often overlooked.

Hydration

40 oz of water a day → up to 54% lower heart-attack risk

A Loma Linda University study of 20,000+ people found women who drank 5+ glasses daily had 41% lower fatal coronary risk; men 54% lower. Most adults chronically drink less than half that.

Cold Exposure

30 days of cold showers → 29% fewer sick days

A 2016 randomized controlled trial in the Netherlands (n=3,018) found cold-shower participants took 29% fewer sick days than a hot-shower control group. Duration matters more than temperature — 30–90 seconds is enough.

Breathing

Box breathing (4-4-4-4) calms you in 90 seconds

Used by Navy SEALs: inhale 4 counts, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Four cycles measurably lower cortisol — the long exhale directly stimulates the vagus nerve, switching on the parasympathetic "rest" system.

Mind-Bending Science

Counterintuitive truths about the world and your body.

Physics

Quicksand physically cannot swallow you

Your body's density (~1.0 g/cm³) is roughly half that of water-saturated quicksand (~2.0). You can't sink past your waist without external downward force. The real danger is being stuck for hours, not drowning.

Biology

You carry 10× more microbial cells than human cells

Around 100 trillion microbes live in and on you, carrying 3.3 million genes versus your own ~20,000. Strictly by cell count, you are mostly not "you."

Animal Biology

Polar bears have black skin under clear fur

Each hair is hollow and transparent, funneling UV light to black skin that absorbs heat. The "white" you see is just light scattering through those hollow strands.

Productivity, Properly Understood

What actually moves the needle, backed by research.

Focus

It takes 23 minutes to refocus after one interruption

University of California, Irvine research clocked the average recovery at 23 minutes and 15 seconds. Every individual notification can cost nearly 25 minutes of real output. Batch your messages instead.

Parkinson's Law

Work expands to fill the time you give it

Allot an hour to a task and it takes an hour. Allot 20 minutes and it often takes 20 — with equal output. Deliberately under-budgeting routine tasks is one of the least-used productivity multipliers.

Memory

Spaced review at 1 day, 1 week, 1 month locks memory in

The Ebbinghaus forgetting curve shows you lose ~70% of new information within 24 hours without review. Three spaced repetitions — at 24h, 7 days, and 30 days — push retention past 90%. Anki and Readwise automate it.
